The Mistly website is no longer just where you download the launcher. Here is what's new today.

## A redesigned status page

The [status page](/status) shows the state of every service Mistly needs: downloads, Microsoft sign-in, skins, Modrinth, CurseForge, mod loaders… The site checks them itself every minute.

For each service: one bar per day over the last 30 days, or one per minute over the last hour, with its uptime and latency. Incidents from the last 90 days are kept in a log.

## Live deployments

On the same page, the latest GitHub Actions runs of the launcher, Mistly Client and the site: what is building right now, what was just released, and what failed.

## A roadmap

The [roadmap](/roadmap) shows what is in progress, what is planned and what has already shipped. We update it with every release.

## A media kit

Making a video or an article about Mistly? The [media kit](/brand) gathers the logo, colours, fonts, screenshots and ready-to-copy descriptions.

## And articles

This page will host release announcements, behind-the-scenes posts and guides. To never miss one, join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/rdQJUgvUh3).
