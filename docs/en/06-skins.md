The **Skin** tab shows your character in 3D in a tall tile. It changes the skin and the cape of your Microsoft account, and the Mistly cosmetics you wear in game.

> [!NOTE]
> Changing the skin or a Mojang cape needs a Microsoft account. Without one, the tab shows a 3D Steve and offers to sign in. See [Accounts](/docs/accounts).

## The 3D character

- **Drag** the character to turn it; the **mouse wheel** zooms.
- **Front / Side / Back** and **Rotate** change the view; **Reset** brings back the front view.
- **Hover** the character: the parts you can change light up in blue, with their name next to the cursor. **Click** one (the head for hats, the cape for capes, the wings for wings, your name above the head for tags) and the list of what can go there opens on the side, while the camera turns to that part.

## Change your skin

1. On your player card, choose a skin file (PNG). The arm model, **Classic** or **Slim**, is detected from the file; you can change it.
2. Check the preview, then click **Apply**.

The skin must be a PNG image of **64×64** or **64×32** pixels.

**My skins** keeps the heads of the last skins you wore or tried: click one to bring it back, then **Apply**.

## Capes

Every cape on your Mojang account is shown with a preview. One click equips a cape; **None** removes it. The change is sent to Mojang right after and applies everywhere you play.

## Mistly cosmetics

One slot per cosmetic shows what you wear:

| Slot | What goes there |
|---|---|
| Cape | The Mistly cape, or the early player cape kept for the first Mistly players |
| Wings | The Mistly elytra |
| Hat | Cloudly, a little cloud floating over your head |
| Tag | The style of your name above your head: **Classic** or **Cloud** |

Mistly cosmetics are only visible in game with [Mistly Client](/docs/mistly-client): you see them, and so do the other Mistly Client players. A cosmetic equipped while the game runs appears right away.

## Fast and offline-friendly

Your skin and capes are loaded in the background when Mistly starts and kept on your disk, so the tab opens instantly. The 3D character pauses while the launcher is in the background or a game is running.

When Mojang limits the number of requests, Mistly keeps showing your last known skin and capes, tells you with a message, and checks again by itself once the limit is lifted.
