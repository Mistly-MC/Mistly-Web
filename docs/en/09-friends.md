The **Friends** tab keeps a list of your friends and shows who is playing.

## Add a friend

Type a **Minecraft username** and click **Add**. Mistly checks with Mojang that the account exists, then the friend shows on a card with their head.

The list is kept on your PC: nothing is sent to your friend, and there is no request to accept.

## Who is online

A friend shows as **Playing with Mistly Client** when they are in game with [Mistly Client](/docs/mistly-client) and its *Mistly network* option on. The list refreshes every minute while the page is open, and **Refresh** updates it at once.

> [!NOTE]
> To see who is online, play once with Mistly Client and a Microsoft account: Mistly remembers your sign-in to the Mistly network. Until then, the status of your friends shows as unknown.

A friend who plays without Mistly Client always shows as offline: Mistly cannot see them.

## Not there yet

Chat, and joining a friend's game in one click, are announced in the launcher but not available yet.
