## How do you want to play?

The first time Mistly starts, the screen is split in two:

- **Premium**: sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft. You get every online server, your skin and capes, Realms and your Minecraft friends.
- **Offline**: just type a username. Good for single player, LAN worlds and servers that accept offline players.

You can switch at any time from your account, at the bottom right of the window. See [Accounts](/docs/accounts).

## The window

The window is a grid of tiles.

| Where | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Top bar | The tabs: **Play**, **Skin**, **Profiles**, **Release notes**, **Texture packs** and **Friends**. Next to them, the **Logs** tile opens the [console](/docs/console) |
| Left column | **Quick play**, then one tile per [profile](/docs/profiles): one click picks it |
| Middle | The page of the selected tab. On **Play**: the Mistly landscape, the latest news and the number of Mistly players online |
| Bottom bar | **Settings**, the version (or the profile) you are about to play, the **PLAY** tile, your account and your avatar |

You can hide the tabs you never use in [Settings](/docs/settings#appearance).

## Start a game

1. **Account.** Check the account shown at the bottom right. Click it to pick another one or add one.
2. **Profile.** *Quick play* uses the shared game folder. Click one of your [profiles](/docs/profiles) in the left column to play with its own mods and worlds.
3. **Version.** Click the version tile to open the version picker: choose an update, a version and a mod loader, then confirm with the **Choose …** button. See [Versions and mod loaders](/docs/versions-and-loaders).
4. Click **PLAY**.

The first time, Mistly downloads the game, its libraries, its assets and the right Java version. The progress shows in the PLAY tile, and a **Cancel** tile lets you stop a download started by mistake.

> [!NOTE]
> Each version is downloaded only once. The next launches start right away.

## While the game runs

- The [console](/docs/console) opens with the game's logs (this can be turned off in the settings).
- **PLAY** is available again as soon as the game has started: you can run several versions, profiles or accounts at the same time.
- The launcher steps aside: its animations pause and it gives its memory back to Windows, so your PC works for the game.

## Next steps

- Create a [profile](/docs/profiles) to keep mods and worlds apart.
- Add [mods, resource packs and shaders](/docs/mods-and-content).
- Try [Mistly Client](/docs/mistly-client) for an in-game HUD and cosmetics.
- Adjust the memory allocated to the game in [Settings](/docs/settings#memory).
