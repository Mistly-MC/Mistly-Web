# Code of Conduct

Mistly is made by a small team and a community of players, translators and contributors. We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their age, experience, background or the language they speak.

This code applies to the Mistly-MC GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to the [Mistly Discord server](https://discord.gg/rdQJUgvUh3).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Minecraft, to modding or to Git.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person. Explain why, and suggest a better way when you can.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and that the final decision belongs to the Mistly team.
- Write in English or French on GitHub, so that the team can read it. Translations are welcome everywhere else.
- Respect people's privacy and time: no repeated pings, no private messages to push a request.

## What is not allowed

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content, or any content that is not suitable for a young audience (many Minecraft players are minors).
- Sharing someone's personal information (address, real name, IP, accounts) without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, scams, and links to cracked launchers, malware or account generators.
- Asking for or sharing Microsoft / Minecraft account credentials or tokens, including in logs.
- Any attempt to sell Mistly or a work derived from it (see the [license](LICENSE.txt)).

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or if you feel unsafe, tell the team privately: send a direct message to a team member on [Discord](https://discord.gg/rdQJUgvUh3). Please include links or screenshots when you can. Reports are kept confidential.

Do not report security issues in a public issue: contact the team privately on Discord.

## What we do about it

Depending on what happened, the team may:

1. send a private reminder of this code;
2. give a public warning;
3. temporarily ban the person from the repositories and the Discord server;
4. permanently ban them.

The team members who handle a report are never involved in it themselves.
